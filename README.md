# Mobile-First Valentine Website v2

## Features
- **Name Entry First:** Captures the visitor's name when they click **Continue** and automatically emails it to `trishirmondal@gmail.com`.
- **Working YES Button:** Displays a personalized **Bengali love proposal**, launches confetti, plays your **Hindi love song**, and emails you that they said YES.
- **Working NO Button:** Switches to a rainy dark-blue mood, displays a personalized **sad note**, plays your **sad song**, and emails you that they said NO.
- **No "Generate Your Own Link" Section.**

## How to Add Your Hindi Love Song & Sad Song
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open the `audio/` folder.
3. Add your favorite Hindi love song and rename it to **`yes-song.mp3`**.
4. Add your favorite sad song and rename it to **`no-song.mp3`**.
*(Note: Even without MP3 files, a built-in romantic/sad melody plays automatically.)*

## One-Time Email Activation (Required)
1. Upload the unzipped folder to **Netlify Drop** (`https://app.netlify.com/drop`) or **GitHub Pages**.
2. Open your live link, type a test name, and click **Continue**.
3. Open `trishirmondal@gmail.com` (check Spam/Promotions too) and click the one-time **Activate Form** email from FormSubmit.
4. Done! Every visitor's name and answer will arrive in your Gmail automatically.
